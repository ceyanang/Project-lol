# Kana-Slayer: Music — Design

Date: 2026-11-22 · Status: draft for approval

## Intent and why

The game has sound effects but no music. The owner wants a soundtrack that sets the mood for each part of the
game and makes fights feel bigger. They heard throwaway samples of four moods and approved them, with one
change: **the boss track was too slow.**

| Where | Mood the owner asked for |
|---|---|
| Title and other menus | chill and zen |
| Adventure (map) screen | calm, but still upbeat and adventurous |
| Normal enemy fights | exciting |
| Boss fights | dangerous (and faster than the sample) |

## Goals

1. Four looping tracks, **generated by code at runtime** (Web Audio), matching the approved samples:
   - **Menu**: ~54 bpm, Japanese in-scale (D E♭ G A B♭), koto-style plucks, low drone pad, breathy shakuhachi on long notes, sparse wind-chime bells, heavy reverb.
   - **Map**: ~112 bpm, yo pentatonic (C D F G A) over C – G – Am – F, triangle bass, soft square arpeggio, pluck + square melody that enters after 4 bars, shaker, woodblock ticks.
   - **Battle**: ~156 bpm, A minor over Am – G – F – E, driving square/saw bass, fast pulse arpeggios, four-on-the-floor kick, snare on 2 and 4, hats, a hook that enters after 4 bars, a snare fill every 4th bar.
   - **Boss**: faster than the sample (which was 92 bpm; target about 126, builder tunes by ear), taiko hits, detuned low drone with an E–B♭ tritone, a low saw "horn" melody in E phrygian, drum rolls into each phrase, dissonant stabs, shakuhachi screams.
2. Each screen plays the right track, with a short crossfade between tracks, and music keeps playing across screens that share a track (no restart).
3. **Reactive in fights**: the battle track gains its hook layer when the combo reaches the first tier (3 correct in a row) and loses it when the combo breaks; elites start with that layer on. The boss track intensifies at the 50% phase change (faster, denser taiko, extra drone). Changes take effect on the next bar line so it stays musical.
4. A **music on/off toggle** on the title screen next to the sound toggles, saved in the profile, separate from sound effects.

## Non-goals

- Recorded or AI-generated audio files, and any new asset download.
- A different track per enemy or per floor.
- Full victory/defeat songs (the existing short sound stingers stay).
- A volume slider, a music player or track selection.
- Changing sound effects or the voice clips (other than keeping them clearly audible over music).

## Anti-goals (failure even if it technically works)

- Music that is louder than, or masks, the kana voice clips and sound effects. Reading must stay the star.
- Music that glitches, clicks, drifts out of time or stutters on a phone, or drains battery noticeably.
- Music that keeps playing when the tab is hidden or the app is in the background.
- Music that plays when the person muted it, or that blocks the first tap (autoplay rules).
- Tracks that sound different on each launch of the same screen without being an intentional variation.

## Constraints

- Offline PWA on phones: **no audio files**; music is synthesized with the existing shared `AudioContext` (`getAudioContext()` in `src/game/sfx.ts`).
- Browsers start audio only after a user gesture; music must start on the first tap/keypress and resume cleanly if the context gets suspended.
- Existing audio code is side-effect UI code (`sfx.ts`); the new music logic should keep its pure parts (the score and the scheduling decisions) testable without Web Audio.
- Tunables go in `src/config.ts`; `PLAN.md` documents the music; settings live in `profile.settings` with a safe default for old saves (`storage.ts`).
- All existing tests, the build and the deploy workflow keep working. No new dependencies.
- Reduced-motion does not apply (audio), but the music toggle must work.

## Approaches considered

| Option | What | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Code-made music (chosen)** | Short note patterns scheduled live through Web Audio | Free, offline, seamless loops, can react to combo and boss phase; synth sound has a quality ceiling |
| B. Generated audio files | Four rendered tracks shipped as MP3 | Richer, but costs credits, adds several MB to the offline cache, loops can click, cannot react to the game |
| C. Hybrid | Files for menu/map, code for fights | Most work; two styles might not match |

The owner chose **A** after hearing throwaway samples rendered in Python.

## Design decisions (made with the owner)

- Approach A; the four moods above; menu zen, map upbeat-calm, normal fights exciting, boss dangerous.
- The boss track must be faster than the sample.
- Everything else in the samples was approved as-is ("everything else is great").

## Proposed details (my calls, change freely)

- **Which screen plays what**: Title, Library, Study focus, Study drills, Skip-ahead Quiz and Rest sites play **menu**; the map and the reward screen play **map**; fights with normal, elite and review enemies play **battle**; boss fights play **boss**; the run-over screen fades the music out so the existing victory/defeat sounds stand alone.
- **Elites** play the battle track with the hook layer on from the start.
- **Volume and ducking**: music sits well below sound effects (master music gain about 0.12 against sfx 0.22) and ducks a few dB while a voice clip plays, so the kana reading is always clear.
- **First launch**: music is on by default and starts on the first tap.
- **Pause when hidden**: music suspends on `visibilitychange` (tab hidden) and resumes when visible, unless muted.
- **Structure**: a pure score module (patterns as data, a `notesForBar(track, bar, layers)` style function), a small scheduler (look-ahead timer, about 25 ms tick scheduling about 100 ms ahead), voice functions (pluck, pad, bell, breath, kick, snare, hat, taiko, bass, lead) built on Web Audio nodes, a generated reverb impulse, and one `Music` controller with `setScene(scene)` and `setLayers(...)`. `App.tsx` picks the scene from the current screen/phase and enemy tier; `Battle.tsx` (or App) feeds combo and boss-phase changes.
- **Boss tempo**: start around 126 bpm with denser taiko; the phase change adds roughly 8% tempo and an extra layer. Builder tunes by ear.

## Left to the builder

- Exact note patterns (start from the approved samples: the scripts that rendered them are in `docs/superpowers/specs/music-reference/`, with every note, tempo and drum pattern; reproduce the same feel, with the boss faster).
- Voice recipes in Web Audio (e.g. pluck from a periodic wave plus an exponential decay), reverb size, per-voice gains, and balancing music against sfx and voice clips on a real phone speaker.
- Crossfade length and how bar-quantised layer changes are scheduled.
- Unit tests for the pure score/scene-mapping logic and a browser check that scenes switch, mute works and nothing plays while hidden.
- Delivery order within the project; a sensible split is (1) engine + menu track + toggle, (2) map and battle tracks + screen routing, (3) boss track + reactive layers, (4) mixing/ducking and docs.
